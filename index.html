Vine Line Privacy Policy

Game: Vine Line: Connect Dots Puzzle (package com.vulturetech.vineline)
Developer: Vulture Tech
Effective date: 6 November 2026

Vine Line is available as an Android app (Google Play, Samsung Galaxy Store, Amazon Appstore) and as a web game (Poki, CrazyGames, itch.io). What is collected depends on the version, so each version has its own section below.

Android app: Google Play and Galaxy Store versions

Vine Line is a puzzle game that works fully offline. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts. If, and only if, you choose Allow when the game asks to share usage stats (or turn on Settings → Share usage stats), the app sends gameplay statistics and crash reports to us through Google Firebase. If you choose No thanks, or never answer, nothing is sent. You can change your choice at any time.

Information stored on your device

To save your game, the app stores these on your device only: level progress, flower ratings, petals, garden items, the daily-puzzle streak, the number of hints left today, your settings (sound and motion), the date the rating question was last shown, your usage-stats choice, and a simple record of game events (for example, which levels were started and finished, and when a hint was used). Apart from the optional usage stats described below, this information never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this game data in your own device backup; that backup belongs to you and is not accessible to us.

Optional usage stats and crash reports (Google Firebase)

Only if you opt in, the app uses two Google services: Firebase Analytics (gameplay statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports). Google processes this data for us as our service provider under the Firebase data processing terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, files or anything you type. The app does not use the advertising ID or the Android ID, does not collect data for advertising, does not use Google Signals, and does not share or sell data to anyone. Analytics advertising features (ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization) are always switched off. All data is sent encrypted (HTTPS).

How to turn it off

Go to Settings → Share usage stats and switch it off. The app then stops sending data straight away, asks Firebase to reset the Analytics identifier for your install, and deletes any crash reports that have not yet been sent.

Android app: Amazon Appstore version

The Amazon Appstore version of Vine Line works fully offline and collects nothing: it has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no analytics or crash reporting, and it does not request internet access. Your game progress, settings and a simple record of game events are stored on your device only. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Ratings and reviews through Google Play

After a happy moment in the game (for example finishing certain levels), the app may ask Google Play to show its standard rating dialog, at most once every 30 days. Whether the dialog appears is decided by Google Play. If you choose to submit a star rating or a written review there, it is handled by Google Play and published on the Play Store under Google's Privacy Policy, just as when you rate an app in the Play Store. If the app is installed from a closed test track, Google Play shares that feedback privately with us instead of publishing it. The app itself never sees your rating or review, and you never have to give one: no reward depends on it.

Ratings

After a happy moment in the game (for example finishing certain levels), the app may ask once whether you'd like to rate it, at most once every 30 days. Not now closes the question; Rate it opens the game's page in the store app you installed it from. Any rating or review you leave there is handled by that store under its own privacy policy. The app never sees your rating, and no reward depends on it.

Web game: Poki, CrazyGames and itch.io versions

When you play Vine Line in your browser, the game itself collects nothing and sends nothing to us: no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts, no cookies of our own. Your progress and settings are saved in your browser's local storage (on CrazyGames, through the CrazyGames save system, which keeps it in your browser or, if you are signed in to CrazyGames, with your CrazyGames account). You can delete it by clearing your browser's site data.

On Poki and CrazyGames, the portal shows the game inside its own website and may show ads before or between levels, and when you choose to watch an ad for a hint. Those ads, and any cookies, consent questions and statistics that come with them, are handled by the portal under its own privacy policy (linked at the bottom of the portal's pages); we receive only overall play and ad statistics from the portal, never information about you. The itch.io version has no ads.

Children

Vine Line is a general-audience puzzle game designed for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has turned on usage stats, contact us and we will delete the related data.

Data retention and deletion (how to delete your data)

Firebase Analytics event data is kept for 2 months, then deleted automatically; summary reports (counts, without identifiers) may be kept longer. Crashlytics crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. To have the data for your install deleted, first switch off Settings → Share usage stats (this resets the Analytics identifier), then email us at the address below; because the data is not linked to your identity, it helps if you tell us roughly when you played. We will delete what we can find within 30 days. Game data on your device stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

The Amazon Appstore and web versions send us no data, so there is nothing for us to delete; clearing the app's storage or your browser's site data removes everything.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what information is handled, we will update this policy and the effective date before that version is released, and describe the change in each store's privacy section.
